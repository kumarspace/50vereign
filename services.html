Our Services

Advisory & Transaction Support

Sovereign Ventures is a boutique investment advisory firm advising clients across capital markets, structured finance, private capital, mergers & acquisitions and cross-border transactions.

We advise businesses, investors and institutions on capital, strategic transactions and international opportunities.

01 · Unsecured Debt / Secured Debt

Capital Solutions Aligned to the Business

Debt requirements differ according to the business, cash-flow profile, assets, existing liabilities and purpose of funding.

Secured Debt

Financing structured against the assets, receivables or other security available to the business, with terms matched to its cash flows.

Unsecured Debt

Financing supported primarily by the strength of the business and its cash flows, where hard collateral is limited or is not the basis of the facility.

We assist clients in evaluating appropriate financing alternatives, including

  • Secured and unsecured debt
  • Structured debt
  • Working capital financing
  • Refinancing
  • Special situation financing
  • Project-related financing
  • Capital structure advisory
  • Debt restructuring

Our emphasis is on understanding the underlying business and its ability to support the proposed financing over the appropriate period.

02 · Private Equity / Quasi Equity

Growth Capital & Strategic Investors

We assist companies seeking growth and strategic capital and investors evaluating private investment opportunities.

Private Equity

Minority or majority equity from private equity funds, family offices and strategic investors, for growth, expansion or shareholder transitions.

Quasi Equity

Instruments that sit between debt and equity, such as convertible instruments and preference capital, where a business needs flexible capital with limited immediate dilution.

Our work may include

  • Capital requirement assessment
  • Investor identification
  • Private equity placement
  • Strategic investor outreach
  • Transaction structuring
  • Financial analysis
  • Investor discussions
  • Negotiation and transaction support

We seek to establish an appropriate fit between the company’s requirements, the proposed structure and the investor’s objectives.

03 · Domestic & International Trade Financing

Financing Across the Trade Cycle

We assist importers, exporters and trading businesses in arranging finance for domestic and cross-border trade, from purchase order through to receipt of payment.

  • Letters of credit
  • Bank guarantees & standby letters of credit
  • Buyer’s credit
  • Supplier’s credit
  • Pre-shipment & packing credit
  • Post-shipment finance
  • Import & export finance
  • Receivables & supply chain finance
  • Working capital for trade cycles
Please note

International transactions may involve additional legal, regulatory, tax and currency considerations. We coordinate with appropriate specialist advisors where required.

04 · Promoter Funding / Private Credit

Flexible Capital Beyond Conventional Lending

Promoters and companies sometimes need capital that conventional bank lending does not readily provide, whether for growth, acquisitions, refinancing or special situations.

Promoter Funding

Financing raised at promoter or holding-company level, structured around the promoter’s shareholding, cash flows and objectives.

Private Credit

Capital from private credit funds, family offices and other non-bank lenders, with terms tailored to the specific transaction.

Our work may include

  • Promoter funding against shareholding
  • Stake acquisition & consolidation
  • Holding-company financing
  • Bridge & event-driven financing
  • Special situation financing
  • Refinancing
  • Lender identification & engagement
  • Structuring & negotiation support

Each structure is evaluated against the promoter’s objectives, existing obligations and the lender’s requirements.

05 · AIF / Structured Financing

Tailored Structures for Complex Requirements

We work with alternative investment funds and structured finance providers where a transaction calls for a tailored structure, whether for a capital-intensive project, an acquisition or a special situation.

  • AIF & fund-led financing
  • Structured debt
  • Project & infrastructure finance
  • Financial modelling
  • Feasibility analysis
  • Debt and equity structuring
  • Financing strategy
  • Investor and lender identification
  • Cross-border capital raising

We advise on selected capital-intensive projects and cross-border opportunities where financing, investment or strategic participation is required.

06 · Mezzanine Capital

Bridging Senior Debt and Equity

Mezzanine capital sits between senior debt and equity in the capital structure. It can fund growth, acquisitions or buy-outs where senior borrowing capacity is fully used and shareholders wish to limit dilution.

Our work may include:

  • Subordinated debt
  • Hybrid & convertible instruments
  • Acquisition & buy-out financing
  • Growth capital with limited dilution
  • Refinancing
  • Capital structure advisory

Each structure is assessed against the business’s cash flows, the position of existing lenders and the shareholders’ objectives.
